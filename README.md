## What belongs here

Include products that meet at least one of these conditions:

- AI is a core part of the product, not just a marketing label.
- The product is a developer, creator, or automation tool commonly used alongside AI.
- The offer provides a real, dated saving on an annual plan, lifetime license, credits, or usage bundle.

Do not add scraped coupon collections, unverified predictions, expired offers as active deals, or products whose only evidence is an affiliate page.

## What every listing includes

Each confirmed listing should show:

- Product and company
- Category and short use case
- Official product and pricing URLs
- Regular price and sale price, in the stated currency
- Discount percentage or savings amount
- Deal type: annual, monthly, lifetime, credits, or bundle
- Coupon code or confirmation that no code is required
- Start and end dates, with timezone
- Eligibility: new users, existing users, students, regions, or other restrictions
- Renewal terms and post-sale pricing
- Last verification timestamp

## Verification labels

- ✅ **Confirmed** — verified against an official vendor source
- 👀 **Watching** — a product to check, but no confirmed 2026 offer yet
- ⏳ **Expired** — historically valid, retained for reference only
- ❌ **Rejected** — incomplete, misleading, or not independently verifiable

Only confirmed offers belong in the active 2026 deal table. Historical and watching data should remain clearly separated.
